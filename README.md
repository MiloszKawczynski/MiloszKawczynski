## About Me

I'm an experienced game developer with 4 years of professional experience and hands-on experience
in Unreal Engine (C++ and Blueprints), GameMaker, and a custom engine built with C++23 and
DirectX 11. I'm passionate about writing interesting, complex systems — AI, design tools, map
generators, and similar systems. 

For years I chaired Digital Night Studio, a game creators' club, where
I built numerous diverse projects across different technologies, including PC, mobile and custom
engine, and took part in many game jams. I won ZTGK in all four technical categories.
